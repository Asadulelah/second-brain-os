# journal/

Dated records of specific events: meetings, calls, milestones, and daily notes.

## What goes here

- Meeting summaries and call recaps: `YYYY-MM-DD Meeting Title.md`
- Daily notes: `YYYY-MM-DD.md`
- Project milestone records
- Decision logs tied to a specific date

## What does NOT go here

- Distilled content from an article or document → that's `summaries/`
- Analysis of a person or venture → update the relevant `people/` or `ventures/` page instead
- Durable strategy artifacts → those belong in `strategy/`
- Concepts and frameworks → those belong in `knowledge/`

## Journal vs. Summary

- **Journal**: a record of something that HAPPENED on a date (event-first)
- **Summary**: a distillation of a SOURCE (source-first)

A Fathom transcript of a call → create `journal/YYYY-MM-DD Call Topic.md` (it's an event).
An article you read and distilled → create `summaries/Article Title.md` (it's a source).

## Format

Journal entries are free-form prose with clear sections:
- **Attendees** (for meetings)
- **Key points / decisions**
- **Action items / open threads**
- **Wikilinks** to every person, venture, or concept mentioned

Wikilink every entity: each mention creates a back-link obligation on the entity's page per [[_system/conventions/back-linking]].

## File naming

- `YYYY-MM-DD.md` — daily note
- `YYYY-MM-DD Topic.md` — meeting or event (e.g., `2026-05-11 CRM Walkthrough.md`)
